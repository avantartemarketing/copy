# Release brief checklist

What has to exist for each release before the emails can be generated. Two lists: the
bespoke copy that someone writes, and the facts that get typed once. Everything else in
every email is locked text or a frame.

## A. Bespoke copy: written once per release, reused across the sequence

| # | Piece | Size | Used in | Written from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook**: who the artist is and why this work | 1 to 2 sentences, 60 words max | Announcement, Last Chance (recap), Now Live, sign-up confirmation | artist facts + artwork facts |
| 2 | **Card line**, one per artwork | 1 sentence, 15 to 30 words | Announcement, Early Access, Last Chance, Now Live, Sustain | artwork facts |
| 3 | **Subject hook** for the Announcement | 3 to 8 words after "Artist – " | Announcement only; every other subject is a frame | artwork facts |
| 4 | **Early Access note**: why this one, to one collector | 1 to 3 sentences, 60 words max | Early Access (LE), Insiders versions | artwork facts + artist facts |
| 5 | **Technique sentence**, only when the edition is unusual | 1 sentence, 35 words max | Announcement, Now Live, Sustain | edition facts |

For a single-work draw release that is about 170 words. For a trio, about 250, because of
the extra card lines. Nothing else in the fourteen-email sequence is written per release.

## B. Bespoke copy: only in some releases

| Piece | Size | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Quote sentence** ("To Gregory, Dream House represents “…”") | one frame filled by hand, because the quote has to fit the grammar | when a usable artist quote exists |
| **Frame description** ("Framer's choice") | one paragraph, 60 words max | time-limited releases with a bespoke frame; used in the Halfway email and framing deep dive |
| **Delay reason** | 1 sentence, 25 words max | only when a delay email is needed |
| **Bespoke headline or footer** ("'Guncaps' decoded", "From his iconic 'Dream House' series") | one line | optional; the default is a frame |
| **Sustain or deep-dive body** | 150 to 300 words, editorial | when the campaign has one. Stays human-written; the template supplies its opener, CTA and footer |

## C. Facts typed once per release

**Release**
- campaign code (`RobertLongo_LE_26`)
- mechanic: draw, timed, or ranked auction
- window for timed editions: 24 hours, 48 hours, one week
- early access: start date and time, length in hours, access code
- public launch date and time (UK)
- close date and time (UK)
- first-purchase framing code, if offered
- fundraiser or beneficiary, if any
- audiences to send to: whole list, past purchasers, engaged sign-ups, Artists & Editions subscribers, exclusions, US and non-US, translations
- advisor name for Insiders sends: Sam, Curtis or Sofiya

**Artist**
- full name, surname, first name
- descriptor ("Brooklyn-born artist", "Korean artist") or none
- the exact words after "collaboration with" (artist, or estate or foundation)
- debut, latest, or second collaboration
- partner estate or foundation, if any
- quote and attribution, verbatim
- three to five artist facts, as bullet points, not prose

**Edition**
- unit: print, sculpture, collectable, tapestry, embroidery
- count, and whether works are available individually, as pairs, or as a set
- medium phrase ("silkscreen print", "20-colour silkscreen", "archival pigment print")
- edition size, for draw releases; timed editions have none
- how it is authenticated: signed by the artist, estate stamp, engraved signature, certificate
- numbered or not
- technique facts: layers, colours, paper stock, varnish, hand-finishing, colourways
- free worldwide shipping, unless not

**Each artwork**
- title exactly as it will appear, and a short title for prose ("Sandy")
- year and series
- three to five facts about the work: what it is, where it comes from, why it matters, anything first-ever or second-ever

**Post-purchase**
- printing update date
- ship windows, framed and unframed
- where it is being made, if not Make-Ready (a sculpture studio, for instance)
- delay phrase ("a couple of weeks") and reason, only when needed

## D. Derived, so nobody types it

Internal email names in the `DDMMYY_TYPE_Campaign - Sub-type` convention; every subject
except the Announcement's; preview text; kickers, headlines and footers; "a trio of new
limited edition prints"; the production sentence; every deadline and dispatch sentence;
"Closes tomorrow at 17:00 UK time"; the framed and unframed variants; the Last Chance
recap; the survey emails; the edition-size confirmation.
