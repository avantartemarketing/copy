# The comms plan, item by item

Source: the Notion Comms database export (11,753 items, 424 campaigns; 4,600 carry copy,
3,300 of them since January 2025) and the default comms plan (`Example_Comms_plans.xlsx`,
one EXAMPLE TL and one EXAMPLE LE campaign listing every item by channel). Counts below are
items with real copy since 2025 unless stated. Where the Notion copy field holds a brief
("Past works carousel", "Hooks for singles + reel if needed") rather than copy, it is
excluded from the counts.

## 1. The answer in one table

Verdicts: **Facts only** means the item can be generated from the release brief with no new
writing. **Template + fragment** means a fixed skeleton with one short bespoke sentence or
two dropped in. **Bespoke** means the system can supply the frame, CTA and closing line but
the content is written for the release. **Instruction** means the copy field is an
instruction to the artist or partner ("add the tracked link, titled 'Get updates'"), which
is itself a template.

Of the roughly 60 item-and-channel cells in the plan, four are bespoke (Sustain editorial
posts, deep-dive emails, paid social scripts, the past-works carousel assets), about twenty
are facts only, and the rest are a template with one fragment dropped in. Across a release
the fragments are the same five sentences reused.

## 2. The fragments a release needs

| Fragment | Size | Written by | Reused in |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hook` | 1 to 2 sentences, 45 words | writer (or bounded AI) | Announcement on IG Main, Insiders, Broadcast, Partner; Now Live; Sustain reminders; the email hook |
| `hook_short` | one sentence, 25 words | derived from `hook` | Twitter, Discord, SMS-adjacent lines, the "just 24 hours" posts |
| `edition_line` | one sentence: technique, signature, numbering, size, beneficiary | assembled from facts, with an optional bespoke technique clause | almost every channel |
| `artist_line` | 1 to 2 sentences in the artist's first-person voice | from the artist interview or quote; the artist edits | Artist IG Post, Artist Twitter, Artist Email, at Announcement, Now Live, Halfway, Last chance |
| `artist_bio` | 2 to 3 sentences on the practice, third person | writer | Coming Soon carousel, Make-Ready teases, paid social |
| `quote` | verbatim | artist or partner | quote-led variants everywhere |

Plus the facts: artist and handles, titles, series and year, edition phrase and count,
mechanic, window, launch and close dates and times, beneficiary and partner with handles,
tracked links per channel, and the story link title.

## 3. Channel style sheet

Measured on real copy since 2025.

| Channel | Median length | Lines | Conventions |
|---|---|---|---|
| IG Main · Post | 413 chars | 5 | @handle on first mention (77%); closes with "Link in bio to …" (32%) or a deadline; `#artistname` as a last line (28%); emoji rare (10%); no links |
| IG Ins · Post | 457 chars | 7 | as IG Main, plus "Signed by the artist. Individually numbered." and "before {date} at {time}" more often; hashtags 40% |
| Twitter | 221 chars | 5 | inline link on the last line: "Get updates: {link}" (30), "Enter the draw: {link}" (19), "Buy a print: {link}" (9); @handles 24%; no hashtags; ⏳ occasionally |
| Artist IG · Post | 339 chars | 7 | first person; tags @avant.arte (84%); "Link in bio to …" (96%); exclamation marks allowed in the artist's voice (7%) |
| Artist Twitter | 222 chars | 4 | first person; "Click the link to …" then the link |
| IG Main · Broadcast | 467 chars | 7 | "Enter now: {link}" (15), "Enter the draw: {link}" (13); emoji bullets ⏳ ✍️ ☝️ accepted (24%) |
| Partner IG · Post | 469 chars | 7 | partner voice ("The Estate is pleased to present…"); tags @avant.arte; closes with the deadline line |
| Arte.bio | 137 chars | 3 | title line, edition line, status line; no punctuation, no links |
| IG Arteinbio · Post | 256 chars | 5 | fixed skeleton with a bullet list of titles |
| SMS | 147 chars | 3 | "{Artist} – {N} hours to go!" then "Orders close … Buy a print: {link}" then "Reply STOP to opt out." |
| Stories (artist, partner, Insiders, main) | instruction | | "Please include the tracked link in your story with the title '{link title}'"; titles used: Get updates (67), Collect the edition (38), Enter the draw (19), Collect now (15), Collect the sculpture (9), Purchase a print, Place a bid, Last chance |

Times and dates: "17:00 UK time" is the dominant form (377 uses) but "5:00pm CEST", "12:00
EDT" and "2pm UK time" all appear, and ordinal dates ("17th") 93 times. Rule for the system:
`HH:MM UK time` and `26 August`, with an optional artist-local time as a second value on
artist channels ("17:00 UK time / 12:00 EDT").

CTA by mechanic and phase: before launch "Sign up for updates" / "Set a reminder" / "Get
updates"; draw open "Enter the draw"; timed window open "Buy a print" / "Collect now";
closing "Closes {date} at {time} UK time" or "closing tomorrow at {time} UK time".

## 4. Item by item, with the template variants

Slots in braces. `{edition_phrase}` is "a new limited edition silkscreen print" or "a trio of
limited edition prints", built from the brief as in the email templates. `{ordinal}` is
debut, latest or second. Optional parts in square brackets.

### Coming Soon

**Twitter** (83). Six variants cover the archive:

1. `Coming soon: {title} by {artist}.` / `{hook_short}` / `Sign up for updates.`
2. `Coming soon – our {ordinal} collaboration with {descriptor} {artist}.` / `{hook_short}` / `Get updates: {link}`
3. `{n} works by {artist} are coming soon as limited edition prints, authorised by the {partner}.` / `“{quote}” {attribution}` / `Sign up for updates: {link}`
4. `We're delighted to be working with {artist} again on {edition_phrase}[ in support of {beneficiary}].` / `Launching soon.` / `Get updates: {link}`
5. Timed: `{hook_short}` / `{title} will be available to collect as a {window} limited edition from {launch_date} at {launch_time}.` / `Sign up for updates.`
6. Artist-voice retweet copy: `{artist_line}` / `Sign up via the link in our bio.`

**Artist Twitter** (16): `My new collaboration with @avant_arte is coming very soon.` / `{artist_line}` / `Click the link to get updates: {link}`; or `I'm pleased to be partnering with Avant Arte for our latest collaboration – {edition_phrase} launching {launch_time} on {launch_date}.` / `Click the link for updates and to be the first to hear more: {link}`.

**IG Main · Post, the past-works carousel** (29 rows, half of them briefs). The imagery is
bespoke, the caption is not: `{artist_bio, opening with the artist's name and @handle}` /
`Our {ordinal} collaboration is on the horizon. Link in bio to register for updates.` /
`#{artistname}`. Timed variant: `{headline line}` / `On {launch_date}, we'll release {title}
by @{artist} as a {window} limited edition.` / `{hook}` / `Link in bio for updates.`

**Stories**: `Please include the tracked link in your story with the title 'Get updates'`.

### Sustain

The one item where most of the archive is editorial: of 165 Sustain posts on the three
Instagram accounts, 97 are one-off editorial pieces, 18 are "a closer look", 15 are "in the
studio" or signing posts, 14 are quote-led, 9 are making-of posts and 8 are reminders. The
system should offer a menu of six formats and leave the rest to the writer:

1. **A closer look** (IG Main, Artist IG): `A closer look at {title} – {edition_phrase} by @{handle}[, launching {launch_date}].` / `{detail fragment, two to three sentences}` / `Link in bio to set a reminder.` or `Link in bio to enter the draw.` Template plus fragment.
2. **Quote-led**: `“{quote}” – {artist}` / `{one or two sentences linking the quote to the edition}` / `Link in bio to …`. Template plus a short fragment.
3. **In the studio / signing** (artist channels): `In the studio signing '{title}' ahead of my upcoming print release in collaboration with Avant Arte.` / `On {launch_date}, {the edition} will be available to collect for {window} only.` / `Head here to get updates: {link}`. Facts only; the imagery is the content.
4. **The making of**: `The making of {title}. Created in our specialist fine art printmaking studio, @make__ready, {technique clause}.` / `Available to collect for {window} only, starting {launch_date} at {launch_time}. Link in bio to set a reminder.` Template plus the technique clause.
5. **Still time** (Twitter, Insiders, Broadcast): `There's still time to enter the draw for our {ordinal} collaboration with {artist} – {edition_phrase}.` / `{hook_short}` / `Enter the draw before {close_time} on {close_date}: {link}`. Facts only.
6. **Launch reminder** (Insiders, Twitter): `Launching {launch_date} for {window} only – {edition_phrase} by @{handle}.` / `{edition_line}` / `Link in bio to sign up now.` Facts only.

Deep-dive emails, storytelling reels, documentary tie-ins, birthday posts and "the
evolution of" posts stay bespoke. The system gives them the closing line and the deadline.

## 5. What the archive says about doing this

- **The team already writes templates by hand.** Drafts carry `[XX] hours to go`,
  `XXXLINKXXX`, `[tomorrow / date] at [time] UK time`, `Opens XXXXXX`. The system makes the
  placeholders real fields.
- **One caption serves several channels.** The Insiders account reuses the main caption
  verbatim; the Broadcast is the caption with an inline link; Twitter is the first sentence
  plus a link line. Generate once, derive the rest.
- **The copy field is not always copy.** IG Main · Post is 97% real captions, Twitter 73%,
  Paid Social 54%, Arte.bio 40%, artist stories 7%. Stories carry instructions; paid social
  carries briefs. The system should model stories as an asset plus on-image lines plus a
  link title, and paid social as a scaffold for a writer.
- **Formats drift the same way the emails do.** Four time formats and ordinal dates in
  social copy; "link in bio" in eleven phrasings. A filter fixes it.
- **Timing is stable enough to template the plan itself.** Coming Soon lands at −39 days for
  a draw and −34 for a timed edition, Announcement at −27 and −21, Early Access at −29 and
  −1, Sustain at −13 and −9, Halfway at +2, Last chance at −1 for a draw and on the closing
  day for a timed edition. The plan can be generated from the launch date.
