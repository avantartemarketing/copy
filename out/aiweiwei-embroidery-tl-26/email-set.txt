Subject: Ai Weiwei – A quartet of new limited edition embroideries
Preview: Launching 28 January. Register for updates.
Kicker: Launching 28 January
Headline: Self-Portrait Embroidery by Ai Weiwei

We're delighted to announce our latest collaboration with Ai Weiwei – a quartet of new limited edition embroideries, offered individually, in pairs or as a complete quartet.

Self-Portrait Embroidery pixelates an image of the artist with a shaved head and a scar, the result of police brutality following a Citizens' Investigation he led into the 2008 earthquake in Sichuan. The defiant self-portrait becomes four textile works of approximately 460,000 stitches each, in the pixelated style of his 2016 toy brick works.

Each embroidery was produced in close collaboration with the artist, translating the pixelated texture and bold presence of the original toy brick works into approximately 460,000 stitches.

· Framed
· Signed by the artist via a sticker at the back
· Individually numbered
· Free worldwide shipping
· Available individually, in pairs or as a complete quartet

The edition will be available to collect for one week only, starting at 14:00 UK time on Wednesday, 28 January.

Click below to learn more and register for updates.
