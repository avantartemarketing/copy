[PARAGRAPH] this release's paragraph, for the advisor's monthly email
From Glenn Ligon comes two new limited edition silkscreen prints, Untitled (White on White) and Untitled (Black on Black).

Revisiting Ligon's landmark Stranger series, the editions translate passages from James Baldwin's renowned essay, Stranger in the Village, into layered compositions where language becomes pure abstraction. Originally made with coal dust and oil stick, the compositions give Baldwin's words a tangible physical weight.

Each print was produced by our printmakers at Make-Ready as a 26-layer silkscreen, capturing the depth and materiality of the original coal dust and oil stick compositions.

The edition will be allocated by a randomised draw, which closes at 17:00 UK time on 9 September.
