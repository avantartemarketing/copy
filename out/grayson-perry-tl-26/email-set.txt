[HEADLINE] The Changeling by Grayson Perry
[BODY]
We're delighted to announce our latest collaboration with Grayson Perry – The Changeling, a new limited edition print, released in support of the Foundling Museum.

The Changeling portrays a child as a symbol of modern youth, exploring how technology shapes today's generations. It reimagines changeling folklore for the digital age, and is intended to be 'quite disturbing'.

To create the edition, our printmakers at Make-Ready translated a new design into a 9-colour silkscreen, capturing the vivid detail of the original drawing.

[FEATURES]
· Signed by the artist
· Individually numbered
· Free worldwide shipping

The edition will be available to collect for one week only, starting at 14:00 UK time on Thursday, 30 April.

Click below to learn more and register for updates.
