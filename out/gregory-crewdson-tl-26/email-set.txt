[HEADLINE] The Dream House series by Gregory Crewdson
[BODY]
We're delighted to announce our latest collaboration with New York-based artist Gregory Crewdson – six new limited edition prints from his iconic Dream House series.

Originally commissioned by the New York Times in 2002, Dream House was shot inside an abandoned Vermont home with a cast including Julianne Moore, Philip Seymour Hoffman, Tilda Swinton, William H. Macy and Gwyneth Paltrow. The series transforms suburban America into a cinematic psychological landscape.

Faithfully translated by our printmakers at Make-Ready, the six editions are drawn from the twelve staged photographs of the original series.

[FEATURES]
· Signed by the artist
· Individually numbered
· Free worldwide shipping
· Bespoke silver aluminium frame available

The edition will be available to collect for 48 hours only, starting at 17:00 UK time on Tuesday, 30 June.

Click below to learn more and register for updates.
