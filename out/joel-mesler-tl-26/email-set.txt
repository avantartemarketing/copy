Subject: Joel Mesler – A new limited edition print in four colourways
Preview: Launching 20 May. Register for updates.
Kicker: Launching 20 May
Headline: I Love You by Joel Mesler

We're delighted to announce our debut collaboration with Joel Mesler – I Love You, a new limited edition print in four colourways.

I Love You is a declaration of love in four colourways, the colour of the text changing across the quartet. Drawing on personal memory and popular culture in equal measure, the work is disarmingly direct, and the title leaves no doubt.

Created in close collaboration with the artist by our printmakers at Make-Ready, the edition unites a 9-colour split fountain silkscreen with an archival pigment overlay, metallic shimmer and high-gloss varnish.

· Signed by the artist
· Individually numbered
· Free worldwide shipping
· Available in four colourways

The edition will be available to collect for 48 hours only, starting at 17:00 UK time on Wednesday, 20 May.

Click below to learn more and register for updates.

CTA: Discover the collaboration

Card: I Love You
Three words, as direct as Mesler gets, in a 9-colour split fountain silkscreen finished with metallic shimmer and high-gloss varnish, in a choice of four colourways.

Quote: “What I put out is an honest reflection of my thoughts and experiences. At this point in my life, I have nothing to hide – the good, the bad, and everything in between.” – Joel Mesler
