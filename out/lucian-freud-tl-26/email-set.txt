Subject: Lucian Freud – A quartet of new limited edition prints
Preview: Launching 14 April. Register for updates.
Kicker: Launching 14 April
Headline: Portraits, 1952–2002 by Lucian Freud

We're delighted to announce our latest collaboration with the Lucian Freud Estate – a quartet of new limited edition prints spanning five decades of the artist's career.

Portraits, 1952–2002 brings together four seminal works spanning five decades of Freud's uncompromising portrait practice, from early intimacy to late self-scrutiny. The quartet is Girl in Bed, 1953; Reflection with Two Children (Self-Portrait), 1965; Double Portrait, 1988–90; and Self-Portrait (Reflection), 2002.

Each print was created in close collaboration with the Lucian Freud Estate by our printmakers at Make-Ready, on 310gsm Hahnemühle German Etching paper, to faithfully preserve the texture, tonal depth and psychological intensity of the original paintings.

· Individually numbered
· Free worldwide shipping
· Created in collaboration with the Lucian Freud Estate and Bridgeman Images
· Available individually, as curated pairs or as a complete quartet

The edition will be available to collect for 48 hours only, starting at 14:00 UK time on Tuesday, 14 April.

Click below to learn more and register for updates.
