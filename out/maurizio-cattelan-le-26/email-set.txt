From Italian artist Maurizio Cattelan comes Window, a new limited edition print, the artist's first print release with Avant Arte.

Window reimagines Cattelan's legendary Comedian, the duct-taped banana, against hyper-realistic natural landscapes, grounding his provocative symbol in serene compositions of subtly absurd beauty.

Each silkscreen print was produced by our printmakers at Make-Ready in four layers, sealed with a matte varnish.

The edition will be allocated by a randomised draw, which closes at 17:00 UK time on 20 February.
