[HEADLINE] Untitled (Sandy, from the series “Men in the Cities”)

[BODY]
We're delighted to announce our latest collaboration with Brooklyn-born artist Robert Longo – a new limited edition silkscreen print.

Longo is renowned for works that fiercely engage with social structures and mass media, long-maintaining that making art is a political act. Sandy is released in support of Vote.org and marks only the second ever edition of Men in the Cities in silkscreen form, the first being our release of Eric last year.

To craft this edition we have been granted access to the artist's original Kodak transparencies, enabling us to create a high fidelity silkscreen edition of the original piece.
