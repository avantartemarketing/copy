[PARAGRAPH] this release's paragraph, for the advisor's monthly email
From Brooklyn-born artist Robert Longo comes Untitled (Sandy, from the series “Men in the Cities”), a new limited edition silkscreen print, released in support of Vote.org.

Sandy is one of the best-known figures from Men in the Cities, and this is only the second time the series has been editioned in silkscreen, following the release of Eric last year.

To craft the edition, our printmakers at Make-Ready worked from the original Kodak transparencies, creating a high fidelity silkscreen of the original piece.

The edition will be allocated by a randomised draw, which closes at 17:00 UK time on 29 September.
