[BODY]
Robert Longo is a New York artist known for monumental charcoal drawings that take their images from film, news and mass media. Men in the Cities, begun in 1979, made him a defining figure of the Pictures Generation, and he has long maintained that making art is a political act.

Sandy is one of the best-known figures from Men in the Cities, and this is only the second time the series has been editioned in silkscreen, following the release of Eric last year.

“It was really important to me that as I got older as an artist, I could still make work that meant something. I wanna make work that matters.”
