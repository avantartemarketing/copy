Robert Longo (@robert_longo_studio) is a New York artist known for monumental charcoal drawings that take their images from film, news and mass media. Men in the Cities, begun in 1979, made him a defining figure of the Pictures Generation, and he has long maintained that making art is a political act.

Our latest collaboration is on the horizon.

Announcing ‘Untitled (Sandy, from the series “Men in the Cities”)’, a new limited edition silkscreen print by Robert Longo (@robert_longo_studio).

Sandy is one of the best-known figures from Men in the Cities, and this is only the second time the series has been editioned in silkscreen, following the release of Eric last year.

Signed by the artist. Individually numbered. Free worldwide shipping. Same dimensions as Avant Arte's edition of Eric. Released in support of Vote.org (@votedotorg).
