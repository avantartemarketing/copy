We're delighted to announce our latest collaboration with Robert Longo (@robert_longo_studio) – ‘Untitled (Sandy, from the series “Men in the Cities”)’.

Longo is renowned for works that fiercely engage with social structures and mass media, long-maintaining that making art is a political act. Sandy is released in support of Vote.org and marks only the second ever edition of Men in the Cities in silkscreen form, the first being our release of Eric last year.

Signed by the artist. Individually numbered.

Link in bio to enter the draw. Closes at 17:00 UK time, 29 September.
