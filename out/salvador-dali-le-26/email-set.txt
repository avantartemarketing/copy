From the Salvador Dalí Estate comes three new limited edition prints bringing together some of Dalí's most renowned paintings.

Originally painted between 1933 and 1934, Millet's Architectonic Angelus, The Spectre of Sex Appeal and The Phantom Cart are prime examples of Dalí's fascination with dreams, desire and the subconscious. Each is charged with surreal and erotic suggestion.

Each print was created in close collaboration with the Salvador Dalí Estate by our printmakers at Make-Ready, and is finished with a bespoke debossed estate stamp.

The edition will be allocated by a randomised draw, which closes at 14:00 UK time on 11 September.
