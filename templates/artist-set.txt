{#
  THE ARTIST SET — the posts and tweets the artist publishes on their own accounts, in their own voice.
  Same fragments as the Instagram set (hook, making); no bio and no quote, which are redundant on
  the artist's own account. Built to the archive's own artist posts: the announcement carries the
  hook; the sustain and the last chance carry the making line; the announcement and the live post
  carry the edition as one sentence, "Each print is signed and numbered, with free worldwide
  shipping", never as a features list, which reads as a shop on an artist's account.
  Voice: artist.voice is "first" (I, my: 63% of the archive's artist posts) or "third" (a studio's or
  an estate's account: "by Grayson Perry, created in collaboration with @avant.arte").
  Avant Arte is @avant.arte on Instagram and named on X. Stories carry no copy in the plan.
  Posts: coming soon · announce · sustain · live · still time · last chance (a draw has no live or still time).
  Tweets: the same phases, the hook on the announcement only, the link in the action line.
#}
