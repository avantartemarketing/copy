  THE EMAIL SET — the emails in the comms plan, from the same brief as the Instagram set.
  Same fragments: hook, making, quote. The bio belongs to the Coming Soon post only.
  Emails: Announcement (TL Non-flow / LE) · Welcome (TL Flow) · Early Access (TL Flow) · Early access (TL Artist PP)
  · Early Access (Insiders, TL and LE, signed by the advisor) · Early/Exclusive access (LE) · Now Live (TL Flow and Non-flow)
  · Halfway (48-hour window) or 5 days to go + 3 days to go (one-week window) · Last chance (TL Flow / LE)
  · First-time collector survey (LE) · Non-purchaser survey (LE) · this release's paragraph for the Monthly Preview.
  The artist's own email is rare and stays hand-written. The same lines live in tool/release-copy.xlsx.
  Each part of the email is on a labelled line, as HubSpot's modules go: Subject, Preview, Kicker, Headline, then the body, CTA, Card, Quote, Footer
#}
